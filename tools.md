## Data portals

- [FungiDB](https://fungidb.org/) — genome, transcriptome, and functional data
  for fungi and oomycetes, with a query interface built for comparative work.
  The best first stop for most questions.
- [MycoCosm](https://mycocosm.jgi.doe.gov/) — the JGI fungal genomics portal,
  and the source of many published fungal assemblies.
- [NCBI Datasets](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/genome/) — assemblies and
  annotation as bulk downloads when you need to work offline.

## Annotation and gene prediction

- [Funannotate](https://funannotate.readthedocs.io/) — a pipeline for annotating
  fungal genomes end to end.
- [gene prediction parameters](https://github.com/hyphaltip/fungi-gene-prediction-params)
  — Augustus training parameters for a range of fungal species, kept as an archive.
- [BUSCO](https://busco.ezlab.org/) — assembly and annotation completeness against
  lineage-specific single-copy orthologs.
