Some of what used to live here is now archived rather than maintained. The
[gene prediction parameters](https://github.com/hyphaltip/fungi-gene-prediction-params)
collected for training Augustus on fungal genomes have moved to GitHub, and the
original 2006–2007 Gene_prediction pages are no longer served here — they
survive only as an [Internet Archive snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20220416081543/http://fungalgenomes.org/data/Gene_prediction/).
Treat both as historical: the underlying tools have changed considerably since.
